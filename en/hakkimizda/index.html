About Us

Orvak Makina
Orvak Makina is a fictional demo machine manufacturer based in Gaziantep. A story that began in a small workshop in 1998 has grown into a plant that exports machining, packaging and welding equipment to three continents.
Engineering driven
A third of the team are design and R&D engineers.
After-sales support
We are with you through remote connection and on-site service.
History

The Orvak Makina journey

From a small workshop to a plant exporting to three continents.

1998

Founding

Founded in Gaziantep as a small lathe workshop with two masters.

2006

First CNC line

We installed our first CNC machining centre and our own series production line.

2013

Industrial-zone plant

We moved into the new plant in the Organized Industrial Zone.

2018

Robotic welding

OR-Weld robotic welding cells joined our product family.

2022

Solar power

We built a 2 MW solar plant on the roof.

2026

Today

We keep growing with exports to 38 countries.
