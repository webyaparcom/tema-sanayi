How long does it take to prepare a quote?

A written quote is sent within two working days for standard models and within a week for custom configurations.

Are the machines CE marked?

Yes. All series are delivered CE marked under the Machinery Directive (2006/42/EC), and the declaration of conformity is supplied with the machine. The documents on this site are samples (DEMO).

Do you ship abroad?

Yes. The quote lists the Incoterms delivery term, freight and insurance as separate items; the user manual is prepared in Turkish, English and Arabic.

Are installation and training included?

Commissioning and operator training are included; a service team is scheduled for on-site installation.

How do spare parts and warranty work?

Machines carry a two-year warranty, and spare parts are supplied from our warehouse for ten years.
